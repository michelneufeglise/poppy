Poppy's own tools

A SearXNG search engine in Docker on this Mac. Set it up once with "npm run searxng" in Poppy's folder (see the README). It asks search engines like Google, Bing and DuckDuckGo for you, with no account. Poppy reads pages on this Mac.

Make a free key at ollama.com/settings/keys. Your searches and the pages Poppy reads go through ollama.com.

Play, pause, skip and "what's playing" work without keys, and run without asking. To play songs by name, make an app at developer.spotify.com (the owner needs Premium) and paste its client ID and secret. Without them, Poppy opens the search in Spotify.
